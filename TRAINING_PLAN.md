# WAYMARK TRAINING PROGRAM
## Complete Training Plan for Josh — Phase 1 (Weeks 1-6)

---

## PROGRAM PHILOSOPHY

This program is built on the **Tactical Barbell Fighter template** — a system designed specifically for combat athletes who need strength but can only dedicate 2 days per week to barbell work. The Fighter template uses a simple wave-loading scheme (75% / 80% / 90% of your training max) across 6-week blocks, which is ideal for someone managing Muay Thai 3x/week, running progression, and corrective work simultaneously.

**Why this structure works for you specifically:**

- **Deconditioned + poor sleep (4-6hr)**: You cannot recover from high-volume programs. Two hard strength days is the ceiling right now. Research shows that sleep under 7 hours reduces myofibrillar protein synthesis by ~19% and raises inflammatory markers by ~25%. Until sleep improves, volume must stay conservative.
- **Upper cross syndrome**: Daily corrective micro-doses (10-15 min) beat 2x/week long sessions. The NASM 4-step corrective protocol (inhibit, lengthen, activate, integrate) is woven into every warmup.
- **Concurrent training compatibility**: Research confirms that muscle hypertrophy is not negatively affected by concurrent cardio, provided you separate hard conditioning from heavy lower body days and manage total volume.
- **Cannabis use (1.5-4g/day)**: THC impairs REM sleep quality and delays recovery. Combined with already-short sleep, this means autoregulation (RPE-based decisions) is critical. Some days you will need to drop intensity 5-10%.

|------|----------|-------------------|---------------|
| Front Squat | 115 lb | 105 lb | 80 lb |
| Bench Press | 125 lb | 115 lb | 85 lb |
| OHP | 80 lb | 70 lb | 55 lb |
| Bent Over Row | 115 lb | 105 lb | 80 lb |
| Deadlift | 155 lb | 140 lb | 105 lb |

> **Note on Front Squat knee pain**: Use flat shoes or weightlifting shoes with a heel (NOT running shoes). Running shoes have a compressible sole that destabilizes the knee. ## DAILY UCS CORRECTIVE PROTOCOL (10-15 min)
### Perform every day, ideally AM before training or integrated into warmups

This follows the NASM 4-step corrective exercise process: Inhibit overactive muscles, Lengthen them, Activate underactive muscles, Integrate into movement patterns.

**Step 1 — INHIBIT (Foam Roll / Lacrosse Ball) — 2 min**
- Pec Minor: Lacrosse ball against wall, 30s each side, find tender spots and hold
- Upper Traps: Lacrosse ball on upper trap against wall, 30s each side

**Step 2 — LENGTHEN (Static Stretch) — 2 min**
- Doorway Pec Stretch: Arm at 90 degrees on door frame, lean through. 45s each side
- Upper Trap Stretch: Ear to shoulder, gentle hand pressure. 30s each side

**Step 3 — ACTIVATE (Strengthen weak muscles) — 4 min**
- Prone Y-T-W Raises: Lie face down, arms overhead. Lift into Y position (10 reps), T position (10 reps), W position (10 reps). Squeeze shoulder blades. Use 2-3 lb dumbbells or bodyweight.
- Chin Tucks (Deep Neck Flexor Activation): Lie on back, tuck chin to make a double chin, hold 5s. 15 reps. Progress to standing against wall.
- Band Pull-Aparts: Light band, arms straight out front, pull apart to chest. 2 x 15. Focus on squeezing rhomboids.

**Step 4 — INTEGRATE (Movement Pattern) — 2 min**
- Wall Angels: Back flat against wall, arms in "goalpost" position, slide up and down. 2 x 10. If lower back arches off wall, bend knees more.

**Progression**: Weeks 1-3 bodyweight Y-T-W. Weeks 4-6 add 2-3 lb dumbbells. Band pull-aparts go from light to medium band at Week 4.

### TUESDAY

#### AM — Strength A (Full Body) — ~60 min

**Warmup (10 min):**
- Jump Rope: 3 min easy
- UCS Corrective Steps 3-4 (Y-T-W + Band Pull-Aparts): 3 min
- Ankle Mobility: Wall ankle stretches, 2 x 10 each side
- Goblet Squat with 3s pause at bottom: 2 x 5 @ 25 lb (groove the pattern)

**Main Lifts — Fighter Template:**

| Exercise | Week 1-2 | Week 3 | Rest |
|----------|----------|--------|------|
| Front Squat | 3 x 5 | 3 x 3 | 2-3 min |
| Bench Press | 3 x 5 | 3 x 3 | 2-3 min |
| Bent Over Row | 3 x 5 | 3 x 3 | 2 min |

> **Front Squat cue**: Elbows HIGH. If wrist flexibility is an issue, use cross-arm grip. Drive knees out over pinky toes. Brace core hard before each rep — big breath, hold it, descend.

> **Bent Over Row cue**: Your forearms are dying because you are likely using a death grip + too much wrist curl. Use straps or hook grip. Focus on pulling with elbows, not hands. Initiate with scapular retraction.

**Accessory Work (Hypertrophy Focus) — 15 min:**

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Weight | Rest |
|----------|-------------|--------|------|
| Dumbbell Incline Press | 3 x 10-12 | Start 25 lb DBs | 60s |
| Cable Face Pulls | 3 x 15 | Light (UCS corrective) | 45s |
| Dumbbell Lateral Raise | 3 x 12-15 | 10 lb | 45s |
| EZ Bar Curl | 2 x 12 | 30 lb | 45s |

> Accessory work uses higher reps (10-15) for hypertrophy. This is where visible muscle gets built. The main lifts build strength; the accessories build size.

**Core Work — 8 min:**

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest |
|----------|-------------|------|
| Dead Hang | 3 x max hold (goal: 30s) | 30s |
| Ab Wheel (from knees) | 3 x 6-8 | 45s |
| Pallof Press (cable/band) | 2 x 10 each side | 30s |

> Replaced Hanging Leg Raise with Dead Hang for now — you need grip strength and shoulder stability before loaded hanging movements. Replaced V-Ups and Russian Twists with Pallof Press — anti-rotation is more functional for fighting and safer for the spine.

#### PM — Run/Walk Progression (C25K Modified) — 25-35 min

**Phase 1 Running Progression (Weeks 1-6):**

This is a simplified C25K adapted for someone already doing MT 3x/week. Only 1 run per week (Tuesday PM) to start. The MT classes provide substantial cardio conditioning — adding more than 1 run risks overtraining given current recovery capacity.

| Week | Protocol | Total Time |
|------|----------|------------|
| 1 | Walk 5 min, then 8 x (1 min jog / 1.5 min walk) | 25 min |
| 2 | Walk 5 min, then 6 x (2 min jog / 1 min walk) | 23 min |
| 3 | Walk 5 min, then 4 x (3 min jog / 1 min walk) | 21 min |
| 4 | Walk 5 min, then 3 x (5 min jog / 1 min walk) | 23 min |
| 5 | Walk 5 min, then 2 x (8 min jog / 1 min walk) | 23 min |
| 6 | Walk 5 min, jog 15 min continuous, walk 5 min | 25 min |

> **Pace**: Conversational. You should be able to speak in full sentences. If you can't, slow down. There is zero shame in going slow — building aerobic base at low intensity is how fighters develop gas tanks. Zone 2 heart rate (roughly 130-145 bpm for your age).

> After Phase 1 (6 weeks), if 15 min continuous feels manageable, Phase 2 adds a second weekly run on Saturday morning before bag work, progressing to 20 min, then 25 min continuous.

  - Round 1: Jab-Cross-Hook, movement only
  - Round 2: Add teeps and low kicks

**Bag Rounds (4-6 rounds x 3 min, 1 min rest):**

| Round | Focus | Key Drill |

| 6 (if energy) | Power Round | Pick 3 best combos, throw them at 80-90% power with full recovery between |

**Cooldown (5 min):**

## PROGRESSION RULES

### Main Lifts (Fighter Template)
1. **After each 6-week block**: If you completed all sets/reps at 90% TM in Weeks 3 and 6, increase TM:
   - Upper body (Bench, OHP, Row): +5 lb
   - Lower body (Front Squat, Deadlift): +10 lb
2. **If you miss reps at 90%**: Keep TM the same. Run another block.
3. **If RPE on 75% week exceeds 8**: Your TM is too high. Reduce by 5-10 lb.

### Accessory Lifts (Hypertrophy)
1. Use **double progression**: Pick a rep range (e.g., 10-12). When you can complete all sets at the top of the range (12 reps) with good form, increase weight by 5 lb next session.
2. Accessories should feel like RPE 7-8. Never grind accessory reps.

### Pull-Up Progression
1. Follow the 6-week band/negative protocol above.
2. Once you hit 1 full pull-up, switch to: 5 x (1 pull-up + 2 negatives), 3x/week.
3. Once you hit 3 consecutive pull-ups, move to 3-4 sets of max reps (leaving 1-2 in reserve).

### Running
1. Only progress to the next week if the current week felt manageable (RPE 5-6 on runs).
2. If a week felt hard (RPE 7+), repeat it.
3. After completing Phase 1 (15 min continuous), add second weekly run on Saturday AM (before skip rope).

### Bag Work
1. Add rounds as prescribed (4 rounds Weeks 1-3, 5 rounds Week 4, 6 rounds Weeks 5-6).
2. Increase power only after technique is consistent — coach feedback from MT class should guide this.

### Deload Protocol
- **Scheduled deload**: After every 2 blocks (12 weeks), take a deload week. Use 60% TM for 3 x 5 on main lifts. Cut accessories to 2 sets. Reduce bag work to 3 rounds.
- **Autoregulated deload**: If you feel destroyed mid-block (RPE 9+ on 75% lifts, or sleeping under 4 hours consistently), take a deload week immediately. Do not push through. You are not in a position to recover from overreaching.

## NUTRITION GUIDELINES

You need to eat to support muscle growth while doing heavy conditioning. At 158 lb with MT 3x/week plus strength training, you are burning a LOT of calories.

### Calorie Target
- **Training days**: ~2,600-2,800 kcal (slight surplus)
- **Rest days**: ~2,200-2,400 kcal (maintenance)
- You will NOT gain visible muscle in a deficit with this training volume. Eat.

### Macros (approximate)
| Macro | Daily Target | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| Protein | 140-160g (1g/lb) | Non-negotiable. This is the single most important nutrition variable. |
| Carbs | 250-300g | Fuel for MT + lifting. Do not go low-carb. |
| Fat | 70-80g | Hormone support, joint health |

### Timing
- **Pre-MT class (6:15 AM)**: Small fast-digesting meal — banana + scoop of whey, or toast with honey. Not heavy.
- **Post-MT (9:00 AM)**: Real meal — eggs, rice, vegetables. 40g protein minimum.
- **Pre-strength (if AM)**: Same as pre-MT. Train on a light stomach.
- **Post-strength**: High protein + carbs within 2 hours. Chicken + rice, or shake + oats.
- **Before bed**: Casein protein or cottage cheese (slow-digesting protein during sleep). This is especially important given short sleep duration.

## SLEEP OPTIMIZATION (CRITICAL)

Your 4-6 hours of sleep is the single biggest limiter on your progress. Research shows this level of sleep restriction:
- Reduces muscle protein synthesis by ~19%
- Increases cortisol (catabolic hormone)
- Impairs neuromuscular function for compound lifts
- Increases injury risk

### Immediate Actions
1. **Set a hard bedtime alarm** — even 30 min earlier helps. Going from 5hr to 6.5hr would be transformative.
2. **Cannabis timing**: If using, consume 2+ hours before bed. THC close to sleep impairs REM quality (the recovery phase). Consider switching to CBD-only for sleep.
3. **No screens 30 min before bed** — or use night mode/blue light glasses.
4. **Cool bedroom** — 65-68F optimal for sleep.
5. **Naps**: If you can get a 20 min nap between AM and PM sessions, do it. Even micro-naps help recovery.

## RPE AUTOREGULATION GUIDE

Because of your recovery limitations, every session should be self-assessed.

| RPE | Description | Action |
|-----|-------------|--------|
| 1-5 | Easy, barely working | Weight too light OR you're very recovered — enjoy it |
| 6 | Moderate, could do many more reps | Target for warmup sets and accessories |
| 7 | Challenging, 3 reps left in tank | Target for 75% TM weeks |
| 8 | Hard, 1-2 reps left | Target for 80% TM weeks |
| 9 | Very hard, maybe 1 rep left | Expected on 90% TM weeks |
| 10 | Maximal / Failure | NEVER train here. If you hit RPE 10, weight is too heavy. |

**Daily Check-In Questions (before training):**
1. How many hours did I sleep? (Under 5 = reduce intensity 5-10%)
2. How sore am I? (7+/10 soreness = swap main lifts for lighter accessories)
3. How is my mood/motivation? (Very low = this might be a deload signal)

## WHAT THIS PROGRAM DOES NOT INCLUDE (YET)

These are Phase 2+ additions after the first 6-week block:

1. **Sprint conditioning**: Your original program had 8x20s sprints. These are excellent but too much stimulus right now combined with MT + running + strength. Add in Phase 2 (Weeks 7-12) on Wednesday AM before MT class, starting with 4x15s sprints.
2. **Second weekly run**: Added in Phase 2 once 15 min continuous is comfortable.
3. **Heavier bag work**: Power rounds with timed rest. Added once base technique is solid (coach feedback).
4. **Weighted carries**: Farmer walks, suitcase carries — excellent for grip, core, and conditioning. Added in Phase 2 as a finisher on strength days.
5. **Plyometrics**: Box jumps, med ball slams — added once base strength is established and bodyweight feels manageable.
